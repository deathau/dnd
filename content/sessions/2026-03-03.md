# omi summary

## Crash and Damage

- Ship rammed by goblin “Omi”, crashes on desert planet
- Everyone takes 20–28 damage; hull has huge hole
- Party stabilises, heals, then takes an 8‑hour long rest

## Desert Planet & Sphinx Trial

- Planet is hot desert with scattered scrap and ruins
- Large distant stone sphinx spotted to the east
- Approaching sphinx, mental contact offers yearly “trial” race
- Party becomes a new faction; must have a sphinx adjudicator

## Meeting Gray and Choosing a Faction

- Small blue sphinx cub “Gray” appears as adjudicator
- Gray’s siblings: Red with goblin raiders on five‑legged toads (SE),\
  Django with “Rangers” bug‑folk with guns and wagons (NE)
- Race is lethal; Goblins raid, Rangers more settled and hospitable
- Party leans toward allying with Rangers, not goblins

## Car, Travel, and Hex‑Crawl

- Magic matchbox toy expands into full chrome car
- Car is slow, uses 1 fuel per hex; found 5 extra fuel later
- Party drives toward Rangers, chooses path through big dune trail
- Gray rides with them as adjudicator; they skip detailed scouting sometimes

## Ruined Supermarket “We Tell”

- Reach ruined, partly powered supermarket “We Tell”
- Evidence world was once advanced, now post‑apocalyptic
- Party scavenges:
  - Vegan Bell Farris finds weird long‑lasting tofu/veg
  - Radnor finds mold‑covered hanging meat, dry‑ages by trimming
  - Hidden basement via freezer drain holds crates of food and 5 fuel

## Next Steps

- Use new fuel and supplies to continue NE toward Ranger town
- Further explore supermarket basement for more loot/ clues
- Plan upgrades for slow car before deadly sphinx race begins
