[[Olgoth]] is on the roof.

The elves shoot ahead, we get ahead one of the toads. A goblin on his bike stalls. [[story notes/npcs/Abacus|Abacus]]' thorny devil is distracted and does not move.
There's a sandstorm ahead. We're navigating through the storm and there is debris everywhere. [[Rogellio]] dodges a wagon wheel. One of the toads is freaking out and spinning through the air and [[Rogellio]] manages to dodge it, hits a ramp and lands back on the road. [[Radnor]] fails at navigation and we miss the road, but bardic inspiration gets us back on track and we break out of the sandstorm. There's a dangerous death valley ahead of us we need to head into.
A toad came past us while we were deciding what to do.
I summon a giant glowing purple tentacle and we grind down it like skateboarding down the edge of a ramp. [[Olgoth]] is holding on for dear life, waving like a flag on top of the car. There's a bunch of car part, bones, rubble, etc at the bottom of the valley. [[Olgoth]] attempts to tie herself to the seat on the top of the car and gets tight.
[[story notes/npcs/Abacus|Abacus]] is catching up, sliding on the tentacle behind us. I unsummon the tentacle when we hit the ground. [[story notes/npcs/Abacus|Abacus]] drops and then skids, but manages to keep going.
We catch up to the toad goblin and [[Astharoshe|Ash]] calls lightning on it. They get shocked, skeleton visible and all, and we end up running over them both. [[Astharoshe|Ash]] flips the goblin the bird as we pass and the goblin flips him off back as he dies.
We turn a corner and there is debris falling, and [[Olgoth]] notices there are three goblins holding TNT falling amongst the debris directly towards us.  Also a catapult toad is falling, too.
[[Caelith Starweaver|Caelith]] generates an orb of fire and [[Astharoshe|Ash]] spreads the fire with a cube of wind to catch all the debris and either burn it away or push it away. One of the goblins is incinerated and the TNT explodes, the other is slammed against the wall. The "toadbuchet" lands directly in front of us. [[Rogellio]] manages to swerve up the side of some of the destroyed cars and get around it. One goblin survives, and [[Olgoth]] throws a fireBOLT at it and ignites the TNT, killing him in an explosion.
More debris comes at us from the side-ish. [[Rogellio]] swerves around and scrapes the side of the car against the wall of the ravine. Luckily, [[Caelith Starweaver|Caelith]] manages to get her limbs back in before hitting the wall.
The wreckage seems to dry up a little as we head out of the ravine and into a narrow raceway with many turns and a sharp fall off the edges. We manage to navigate it successfully and [[Astharoshe|Ash]]'s mage hand is being used as a windscreen wiper.
We hit a Nadir (the lowest point of the valley) with a tight turn and narrow road. We scrape/grind against the wall, and [[Velfaris]] attempts to use his magical quaterstaff to push the car away from the wall. It doesn't work very well. [[Olgoth]] fires one of our three grapple shots and manages to hit a section of stalagtite and manage to whip the car back towards the track. [[Rogellio]] puts his foot down as he tries to maintain control of the vehicle, while [[Olgoth]] is attempting to detach the grappling hook. The car does a wheelie and manages to detach the hook and we slam back down and keep going.
[[Grey]] is in the boot. [[Grey]] pushes herself up behind [[Velfaris]] to make herself known, right as we're passing a sphinxes forbidden sign. We tell her to get her head back down.
There is a goblin and a ranger directly ahead of us as we enter a cave which seems to have pools of acid sections where there is and isn't railings. The cave is lit by glowing acid and glowing fungus. [[Caelith Starweaver|Caelith]] shoots the goblin with her crossbow (pre-poisoned with scorpion venom). [[Astharoshe|Ash]]'s mage hand flicks the goblin's head, and he turns to see a giant purple tentacle slap him and push him into an acid pool.
We make it around the acid and into a really dark cave. [[Olgoth]] and others light torches, and I touch the bonnet to cast Light and light the bonnet up so we can see.
We crash into a low stalactite though.
[[Rogellio]] flies through the windscreen and hits the stalactite. [[Velfaris]] manages to help him enough to land back on the bonnet, rather than onto the road. [[Rogellio]] asks for someone else to drive and [[Grey]] volunteers. We generally don't like that idea, but argue to nominate another driver. [[Rogellio]] and [[Olgoth]] decide to swap, so now [[Olgoth]] is driving and [[Rogellio]] is harnessed on the roof for the grapple gun.
[[Olgoth]] take off and we leave the cave and head towards a wooden rope bridge.
Everyone hurredly puts out their torches.
[[story notes/npcs/Abacus|Abacus]] is on the other side of the rope bridge.
As we navigate across the bridge, [[Rogellio]] notices that one of the pillars has collapsed, and there's a corpse of an Astral Elf underneath. Its limbs are no longer attached.
There is also a hoverboard nearby, so [[Caelith Starweaver|Caelith]] holds [[Astharoshe|Ash]] out the window to attempt to scoop up the hoverboard. [[Caelith Starweaver|Caelith]] swings him straight into it, and he casts a fireball back at the bridge to take it out.
We're in a desert area with sand dunes with lots more skeletons and debris around. We drive on the compacted sand of this scrapyard. Ahead, we see a green patch with a small lake and trees - an oasis. It seems a little suspicious to the party.
[[story notes/npcs/Abacus|Abacus]] is on the side of the road and his mount is exhausted. We make it to the oasis and stop to take a look.
[[Elnor Starfell]] is there waiting for us, and makes a vague comment about being glad about not having to do the next thing without us.
