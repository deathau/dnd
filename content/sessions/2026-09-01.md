A wild Elder Malestrom appears!
[[Rogellio]] has an idea to summon instant oats, but it's not his turn.
[[Olgoth]] fires her bow at it. The arrow passes through it.
The monster spins and summons four whirlpools rising above the lake at the corners, then charges to the edge of the lake and subsumes [[Astharoshe|Ash]]. He "whelm"s [[Astharoshe|Ash]].
[[Velfaris]] pulls him out with some bardic inspiration from [[Rogellio]] and backs up a little.
[[Rogellio]] casts disintegrate and damages it. A hole is blasted through it, which then seals back up with water.
[[Caelith Starweaver|Caelith]] traps it in an illusory prison
[[Elnor Starfell]] uses her hoverboard and magic sword to slice along the creature's back. It rears out of the illusory prison and takes damage. She then slices through one of the whirlpools and dispels it.
[[Radnor Character Sheet|Radnor]] Eldritch Blasts another of the whirlpools, misses twice bit hits with the third and shrinks it a bit. Then he summons a tentacle to hit it and dispel it entirely. The creature reacts in pain.
[[Astharoshe|Ash]] tried to lightning strike the creature but missed, so threw a rock instead and did basically nothing to it. He throws a middle finger and the creature throws a fin back.
[[Grey]] claws the creature in the face and leaps back.
[[Olgoth]] runs to car and fires a harpoon across the lake at another whirlpool, but it does nothing. It seems it needs magical attacks to dispel the whirlpools.
The creature subsumes [[Radnor Character Sheet|Radnor]] and slams him.
[[Velfaris]] leaps and hits it with his staff and a flurry of blows, but misses the blows.
[[Caelith Starweaver|Caelith]] psychically links herself to the monster and attempts to command it to stand down and go to sleep. It manages to resist the command but remains linked.
[[Elnor Starfell]] hovers over the center of the lake where the treasure is, and is attempting to pick the lock.
[[Radnor Character Sheet|Radnor]] teleports himself, [[Grey]], [[Velfaris]] and [[Rogellio]] to the other side of the lake and re-summons the tentacle to damage one of the remaining whirlpools, which shrinks and hurts the creature a little.
The creature rears up and makes for [[Elnor Starfell]], subsuming her.
[[Astharoshe|Ash]] casts lightning at the creature, which somehow manages to misses the creature but hits [[Elnor Starfell|Elnor]] and singes her.
[[Astharoshe|Ash]] throws another rock, but ends up dropping it on his own foot.
[[Grey]] decides to go for the whirlpool and slashes it, which counts as a magical attack because Sphinxes are magic, and dispels the whirlpool.
[[Olgoth]] casts chromatic orb to blast the creature.
The malestrom creates a giant whirlpool which catches [[Caelith Starweaver|Caelith]] and [[Elnor Starfell|Elnor]] ([[Astharoshe|Ash]] manages to avoid it). [[Caelith Starweaver|Caelith]] and [[Elnor Starfell|Elnor]] are whelmed.
[[Caelith Starweaver|Caelith]] throws [[Elnor Starfell|Elnor]] a potion while caught in the malestrom.
[[Velfaris]] attacks the last remaining small whirlpool, which shrinks but does not dissipate.
[[Rogellio]] unpops a couple of buttons on his shirt then breaks out of his skin into his werewolf form, rushes the whirlpool and bites it and slashes at it.
The creature uncoils itself and drops both [[Caelith Starweaver|Caelith]] and [[Elnor Starfell|Elnor]] as it squirms in pain from the loss of the whirlpools.
[[Caelith Starweaver|Caelith]] attacks the monster with her spear. Its volume is been quite reduced by now.
[[Elnor Starfell|Elnor]] teleports the monster off into the distance, where water simply splashes against the dunes.

Elder Malestrom was defeated!
