In the age of sails and storms, when the horizon was a canvas painted with the colors of adventure and treachery, there lived a fearsome pirate known as Raedanoran. Born amidst a clutch of dragons, he was a Dragonborn, a creature part dragon, part man. From the moment he drew breath, he was an outcast among his kin. The other dragons, fearful of his distinctly un-dragon-like appearance, deemed him a blemish upon their lineage. When the other dragons began to learn to fly, they cruelly bullied their wingless brother. In a cruel twist of fate, they cast him off a cliff, sending him plummeting into the churning waters below.

Raedanoran's cries were swallowed by the waves, but the sea had other plans for him. Against all odds a crew of ruthless pirates, known as the Black Tide, discovered the young Dragonborn struggling against the currents. They pulled him aboard their ship, the _Tempest's Fury_, and raised him as one of their own. The crew, hardened by years of plunder and peril, taught him the ways of the sea, the art of swordplay, and the thrill of the chase. Raedanoran, with his draconic heritage, quickly became a fearsome figure among them, his strength and ferocity earning him the respect of even the most seasoned pirates.

However, the sea is a fickle mistress. In a fierce battle against a rival crew, Raedanoran fought valiantly, but fate dealt him a cruel hand. A cannonball struck his leg, shattering it beyond repair. The crew, unable to save the limb, fashioned a crude wooden peg for him, and he continued to sail the seas, a fearsome pirate with a fierce reputation, but now with a reminder of his mortality.

Yet, the tides of destiny were not finished with Raedanoran. One fateful night, as a storm raged and the waves towered like mountains, the _Tempest's Fury_ was ironically caught in the grip of a furious tempest. The ship was tossed like a toy, and Raedanoran found himself thrown overboard, swallowed by the dark waters. As he sank into the abyss, he felt his life slipping away, the cold embrace of death wrapping around him.

In that moment of despair, he encountered the Fathomless, an unknowable entity that dwelled in the depths of the ocean. The Fathomless, a being of ancient power and mystery, offered Raedanoran a choice: to embrace the darkness of the sea and gain unimaginable power, or to succumb to the depths and be lost forever. With his last breath, Raedanoran accepted the pact, feeling the surge of magic coursing through him as he was pulled from the brink of death.

When he awoke, he found himself on the shore, the storm having passed. The crew of the _Tempest's Fury_ had searched for him, but he was reborn, transformed by the pact he had made. No longer just a pirate, Raedanoran had become a warlock, wielding the dark powers granted to him by the Fathomless.

With his newfound abilities (and many questions about his fateful encounter) Raedanoran chose to leave the life of piracy behind. He donned a new identity, one that would allow him to explore the mysteries of the world and the depths of his own soul. He became a seeker of knowledge,  leaving the sea behind - the only home he'd ever known - to wander the land start his life anew.

And so, the tales of the fearsome pirate Raedanoran faded into legend, while the story of Radnor the warlock had just begun...

## After

I left the infinite ocean behind for my new life on land. But I was adrift; aimless. I found myself pulled along by the current by a new group of companions. Before I even knew what had happened, we left the world behind. My understanding of the world was shattered and expanded. What I had once considered the infinite ocean was nothing but a blanket wrapped around a ball of dirt. One of many. Many worlds in a black expanse dotted with stars. **Wildspace**.

And not just worlds. We end up on the Rock of Bral: An Asteroid with a city. An asteroid that _is_ a city. Entire new races of peoples not seen on [[Faerûn]]. My group of companions and I help kickstart a revolution and embark on a new quest to discover the hidden treasure of [[Captain Flint]], previous owner of the ship we find ourselves on, [[The Glave]]. I also find myself contacted by the Fathomless, the ancient entity to which I owe my very existence, not to mention my powers - which continue to grow in strength. It, too, seeks the treasure, apparently containing some forbidden knowledge my patron requires to obtain its own freedom.

Having a goal renews my focus, and I realise just how adrift I've been since washing up on the shore. I'm still being pulled along in the wake of my companions, but this time with a destination in mind, and hands on the wheel. I begin learning the art of **Spelljamming** - navigating the ship through wildspace. I begin to keep a journal of my adventures. It turns out that some of the people my group and I met on Faerûn are out here in wildspace, too, with goals and ambitions far beyond a single world.

On the planet Thay, we are witness to a violent coup in an ancient vampire family whose patriarch has been missing for three hundred years. A patriarch whom we had previously encountered back on Faerûn. No doubt we will cross paths with him (and his younger brother) again some day.

And my understanding of reality has expanded once again. We are invited to attend a ball that is not just on another world, but in another system. I am coached on how to navigate the barrier between the celestial orb of wildspace and the wider **Astral Ocean** - A cosmic sea where the traditional laws of physics and magic no longer apply as previously understood. Weeks pass by in mere moments; ageing and biological functions seem to freeze; weird and wonderful creatures beyond understanding seem to live here.

It is vast. Truly infinite. It reminds me of the feeling I once had sailing the oceans of Faerûn. Oceans which feel laughably small and insignificant now, given my expanded understanding of reality.

When we left Faerûn, my understanding of the universe was shattered and I was left feeling overwhelmed. But now that understanding has been shattered again and I can feel the pieces start to pull together. I once felt at home on the high seas of Faerûn. I can sense that feeling returning on the Astral Ocean.

The Fathomless didn't just save me from a watery death. It has opened my eyes and delivered me a true home among the stars. Furthermore, I can begin to see how what I once thought of as a vast, infinite sea of water could be considered a tiny prison cell compared to the mind-boggling expanse of the Astral Ocean. I have renewed purpose now. I will find the lost treasure of Captain Flint. I will discover the knowledge required to free my Patron from its prison. Beyond that, who knows? The universe is infinite...
