# Balinor

_Source: Eberron: Rising from the Last War p. 141_
![The Sovereign Host](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/5etools-mirror-3/5etools-img/main/deities/ERLW/The%20Sovereign%20Host.webp#symbol)

- **Alignment**: Unaligned
- **Category**: The Sovereign Host
- **Domains**: Nature, War
- **Pantheon**: Eberron
- **Province**: Beasts, the hunt
- **Symbol**: Pair of antlers _or_ green dragon

Balinor is the Sovereign of Horn and Hunt. He guides both the beast and the hunter, and he is the patron of those who walk on the edge of civilization and the natural world.
