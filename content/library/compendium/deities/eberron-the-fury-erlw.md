# The Fury

_Source: Eberron: Rising from the Last War p. 141_
![The Dark Six](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/5etools-mirror-3/5etools-img/main/deities/ERLW/The%20Dark%20Six.webp#symbol)

- **Alignment**: Unaligned
- **Category**: The Dark Six
- **Domains**: War
- **Pantheon**: Eberron
- **Province**: Passion, revenge
- **Symbol**: Winged wyrm with woman's head and arms

The Fury governs both passion and revenge, rage and despair. She offers revenge to those who have been wronged, but her vengeance often leads to suffering.
