# Brooch of Shielding

_Wondrous item, major, uncommon (requires attunement)_

While wearing this brooch, you have resistance to force damage, and you have immunity to damage from the [magic missile](compendium/spells/magic-missile.md) spell.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 156. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>_
