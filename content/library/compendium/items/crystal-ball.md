# Crystal Ball

_Wondrous item, major, very rare (requires attunement)_

- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

This crystal ball is about 6 inches in diameter. While touching it, you can cast the [scrying](compendium/spells/scrying.md) spell (save DC 17) with it.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 159. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>_
