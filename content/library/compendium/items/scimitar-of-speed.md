# Scimitar of Speed

_Weapon ([scimitar](compendium/items/scimitar.md)), major, very rare (requires attunement)_

- **Damage**: 1d6 slashing
- **Properties**: [Finesse](rules/item-properties.md#Finesse), [Light](rules/item-properties.md#Light)
- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. In addition, you can make one attack with it as a bonus action on each of your turns.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 199. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>_
