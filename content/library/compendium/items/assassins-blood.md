# Assassin's Blood

_Adventuring gear, poison (ingested)_

- **Properties**: [Poison](rules/item-properties.md#Poison)
- **Cost**: 150 gp

A creature subjected to this poison must make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it takes `dice:1d12|noform|noparens|avg|text(6)` (`1d12`) poison damage and is [poisoned](rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) for 24 hours. On a successful save, the creature takes half damage and isn't [poisoned](rules/conditions.md#Poisoned).

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 258. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>_
