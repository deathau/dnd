# Winter's Dark Bite

_Weapon ([greatsword](compendium/items/greatsword.md)), uncommon_

- **Damage**: 2d6 slashing
- **Properties**: [Heavy](rules/item-properties.md#Heavy), [Two-Handed](rules/item-properties.md#Two-Handed)
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

This greatsword is made of an unknown black metal. In most cases, it works as a [+1 greatsword](compendium/items/1-weapon.md). But when used against a [thessalhydra](compendium/bestiary/monstrosity/thessalhydra-hftt.md), it works as a [+3 greatsword](compendium/items/3-weapon.md). While in the Upside Down, it works as a +4 greatsword.

_Source: Hunt for the Thessalhydra p. 4_
