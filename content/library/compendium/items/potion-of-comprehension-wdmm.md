# Potion of Comprehension

_Potion, common_

When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of a [comprehend languages](compendium/spells/comprehend-languages.md) spell for 1 hour. This liquid is a clear concoction with bits of salt and soot swirling in it.

_Source: Waterdeep: Dungeon of the Mad Mage p. 62_
