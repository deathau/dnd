# Dagger of Blindsight

_Weapon ([dagger](compendium/items/dagger.md)), rare (requires attunement)_

- **Damage**: 1d4 piercing
- **Range**: 20/60
- **Properties**: [Finesse](rules/item-properties.md#Finesse), [Light](rules/item-properties.md#Light), [Thrown](rules/item-properties.md#Thrown)
- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This rare magic item requires attunement. A creature attuned to it gains [blindsight](rules/senses.md#Blindsight) out to a range of 30 feet. The dagger has a saw-toothed edge and a black pearl nested in its pommel.

_Source: Waterdeep: Dungeon of the Mad Mage p. 86_
