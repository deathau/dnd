# Propeller Helm

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by a small humanoid)_

While worn, the helm allows its wearer to use an action to cast the [levitate](compendium/spells/levitate.md) spell, requiring no components. The helm's propeller spins and whirs loudly until the spell ends. Each time the spell ends, there is a 50 percent chance that the helm loses its magic and becomes nonmagical.

_Source: Waterdeep: Dungeon of the Mad Mage p. 251_
