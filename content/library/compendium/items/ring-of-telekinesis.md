# Ring of Telekinesis

_Ring, major, very rare (requires attunement)_

While wearing this ring, you can cast the [telekinesis](compendium/spells/telekinesis.md) spell at will, but you can target only objects that aren't being worn or carried.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 193. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>_
