# Torpor

_Adventuring gear, poison (ingested)_

- **Properties**: [Poison](rules/item-properties.md#Poison)
- **Cost**: 600 gp

A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or become [poisoned](rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) for `dice:4d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`4d6`) hours. The [poisoned](rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) creature is [incapacitated](rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated).

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 258. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>_
