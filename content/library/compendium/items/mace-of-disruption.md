# Mace of Disruption

_Weapon ([mace](compendium/items/mace.md)), major, rare (requires attunement)_

- **Damage**: 1d6 bludgeoning
- **Weight**: 4.0 lbs.

When you hit a fiend or an undead with this magic weapon, that creature takes an extra `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`) radiant damage. If the target has 25 hit points or fewer after taking this damage, it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be destroyed. On a successful save, the creature becomes [frightened](rules/conditions.md#Frightened) of you until the end of your next turn.

While you hold this weapon, it sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 179. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>_
