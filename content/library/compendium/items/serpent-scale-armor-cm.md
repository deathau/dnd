# Serpent Scale Armor

_Medium armor ([scale mail](compendium/items/scale-mail.md)), uncommon_

- **Armor Class**: 14 + Dex modifier (max of +2)
- **Weight**: 45.0 lbs.

This suit of magic armor is made from shimmering scales. While wearing it, you can apply your full Dexterity modifier (instead of a maximum of +2) when determining your Armor Class. In addition, this armor does not impose disadvantage on your Dexterity ([Stealth](rules/skills.md#Stealth)) checks.

_Source: Candlekeep Mysteries p. 98_
