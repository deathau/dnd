# Wand of Magic Detection

_Wand, minor, uncommon_

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This wand has 3 charges. While holding it, you can expend 1 charge as an action to cast the [detect magic](compendium/spells/detect-magic.md) spell from it. The wand regains `dice:1d3|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d3`) expended charges daily at dawn.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 211. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span> and the Basic Rules (2014)_
