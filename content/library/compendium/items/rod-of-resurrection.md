# Rod of Resurrection

_Rod, major, legendary (requires attunement by a cleric, druid, or paladin)_

- **Weight**: 2.0 lbs.

The rod has 5 charges. While you hold it, you can use an action to cast one of the following spells from it: [heal](compendium/spells/heal.md) (expends 1 charge) or [resurrection](compendium/spells/resurrection.md) (expends 5 charges).

The rod regains 1 expended charge daily at dawn. If the rod is reduced to 0 charges, roll a `dice:d20|noform|noparens|avg` (`d20`). On a 1, the rod disappears in a burst of radiance.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 197_
