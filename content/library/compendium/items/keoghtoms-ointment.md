# Keoghtom's Ointment

_Wondrous item, minor, uncommon_

This glass jar, 3 inches in diameter, contains `dice:1d4+1|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4 + 1`) doses of a thick mixture that smells faintly of aloe. The jar and its contents weigh ½ pound.

As an action, one dose of the ointment can be swallowed or applied to the skin. The creature that receives it regains `dice:2d8+2|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d8 + 2`) hit points, ceases to be [poisoned](rules/conditions.md#Poisoned), and is cured of any disease.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 179. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span> (as "Restorative Ointment") and the Basic Rules (2014)_
