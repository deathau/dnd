# Gem of Seeing

_Wondrous item, major, rare (requires attunement)_

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This gem has 3 charges. As an action, you can speak the gem's command word and expend 1 charge. For the next 10 minutes, you have [truesight](rules/senses.md#Truesight) out to 120 feet when you peer through the gem.

The gem regains `dice:1d3|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d3`) expended charges daily at dawn.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 172. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>_
