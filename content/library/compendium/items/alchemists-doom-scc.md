# Alchemist's Doom

_Adventuring gear_

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This sticky, adhesive fluid ignites when exposed to air. As an action, you can throw this flask up to 20 feet, shattering it on impact. Make a ranged attack against a creature or object, treating the alchemist's doom as an improvised weapon. On a hit, the target takes `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg|text(7)` (`2d6`) fire damage at the start of each of its turns. A creature can end this damage by using its action to make a DC 10 Dexterity check to extinguish the flames.

_Source: Strixhaven: A Curriculum of Chaos p. 179_
