# Hunting Trap

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 5 gp
- **Weight**: 25.0 lbs.

When you use your action to set it, this trap forms a saw-toothed steel ring that snaps shut when a creature steps on a pressure plate in the center. The trap is affixed by a heavy chain to an immobile object, such as a tree or a spike driven into the ground. A creature that steps on the plate must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take `dice:1d4|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d4`) piercing damage and stop moving. Thereafter, until the creature breaks free of the trap, its movement is limited by the length of the chain (typically 3 feet long). A creature can use its action to make a DC 13 Strength check, freeing itself or another creature within its reach on a success. Each failed check deals 1 piercing damage to the trapped creature.

_Source: Player's Handbook p. 152. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span> and the Basic Rules (2014)_
