# Mask of the Beast

_Wondrous item, uncommon_

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This wooden mask is shaped in the likeness of a beast's visage and has 3 charges. While wearing the mask you can expend 1 charge and use the mask to cast the [animal friendship](compendium/spells/animal-friendship.md) spell as an action. The mask regains all expended charges at dawn.

_Source: Tomb of Annihilation p. 207_
