# Antitoxin (vial)

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 50 gp

A creature that drinks this vial of liquid gains advantage on saving throws against poison for 1 hour. It confers no benefit to undead or constructs.

_Source: Player's Handbook p. 151. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span> and the Basic Rules (2014)_
