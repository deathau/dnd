# Hat of Disguise

_Wondrous item, major, uncommon (requires attunement)_

While wearing this hat, you can use an action to cast the [disguise self](compendium/spells/disguise-self.md) spell from it at will. The spell ends if the hat is removed.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 173. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>_
