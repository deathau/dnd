# Clothing, cold weather

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 10 gp
- **Weight**: 5.0 lbs.

This outfit consists of a heavy fur coat or cloak over layers of wool clothing, as well as a fur-lined hat or hood, goggles, and fur-lined leather boots and gloves.

As long as cold weather clothing remains dry, its wearer automatically succeeds on saving throws against the effects of [extreme cold](compendium/traps-hazards/extreme-cold.md).

_Source: Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden p. 20_
