# Two-Weapon Fighting

_Source: Player's Handbook, Xanathar's Guide to Everything p. 15. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>_

When you engage in two-weapon fighting, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of the second attack.
