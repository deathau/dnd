***Multiattack.*** Becklin makes two melee attacks.

***Heavy Crossbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d10) piercing damage.

***Leadership (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).*** For 1 minute, Becklin can utter a special command or warning whenever a nonhostile creature that it can see within 30 feet of it makes an attack roll or a saving throw. This effect ends if Becklin is [incapacitated](rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated).
