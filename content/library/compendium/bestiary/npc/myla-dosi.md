***Inquiring Mind (1/Day).*** Myla can cast [detect magic](compendium/spells/detect-magic.md), requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability.

***Pack Tactics.*** Myla has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of its allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [incapacitated](rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated).

***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, Myla has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom ([Perception](rules/skills.md#Perception)) checks that rely on sight.

***Alchemical Flame (Recharge 6).*** Myla unleashes fire in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) fire damage on a failed saving throw, or half as much damage on a successful one.
