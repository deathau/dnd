## Traits

***Bonus Proficiencies.*** Levna is proficient with simple and martial weapons, shields, and all armor.

***Pack Tactics.*** Levna has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of her allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [incapacitated](rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated).

***Second Wind.*** Levna can use a bonus action on her turn to regain hit points equal to 1d10 + her level. Once she uses this feature, she can't use it again until she finishes a short or long rest.

***Improved Critical.*** Levna's attack rolls now score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.

***Extra Attack.*** Levna can attack twice, instead of once, whenever she takes the [Attack](rules/actions.md#Attack) action on her turn.

***Battle Readiness.*** Levna has advantage on initiative rolls.

***Improved Defense.*** Levna's Armor Class increases by 1.

***Indomitable.*** Levna can reroll a saving throw that she fails, but she must use the new roll. Once she uses this feature, she can't use it again until she finishes a long rest.

## Actions

***Greatsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.

## Reactions

***Protection.*** When a creature Levna can see within 5 feet of her is targeted by an attack, she can impose disadvantage on the attack roll if she can see the attacker and she is wielding a melee weapon.
