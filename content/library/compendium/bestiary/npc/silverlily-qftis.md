## Traits

***Innate Spellcasting.*** Silverlily's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 14). Silverlily can innately cast the following spells, requiring no components:

**At will:** [detect evil and good](compendium/spells/detect-evil-and-good.md), [druidcraft](compendium/spells/druidcraft.md), [pass without trace](compendium/spells/pass-without-trace.md)

***Charge.*** If Silverlily moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a horn attack on the same turn, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked [prone](rules/conditions.md#Prone).

***Magic Resistance.*** Silverlily has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Magic Weapons.*** Silverlily's weapon attacks are magical.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** Silverlily makes two attacks: one with its hooves and one with its horn.

***Hooves.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

***Horn.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.

***Healing Touch (3/Day).*** Silverlily touches another creature with its horn. The target magically regains 11 (2d8 + 2) hit points. In addition, the touch removes all diseases and neutralizes all poisons afflicting the target.

***Teleport (1/Day).*** Silverlily magically teleports itself and up to three willing creatures it can see within 5 feet of it, along with any equipment they are wearing or carrying, to a location Silverlily is familiar with, up to 1 mile away.

## Legendary Actions

Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, Silverlily can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Silverlily regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.

***Hooves.*** Silverlily makes one attack with its hooves.

***Shimmering Shield (Costs 2 Actions).*** Silverlily creates a shimmering, magical field around itself or another creature it can see within 60 feet of it. The target gains a +2 bonus to AC until the end of Silverlily's next turn.

***Heal Self (Costs 3 Actions).*** Silverlily magically regains 11 (2d8 + 2) hit points.
