***Silver Greatsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) psychic damage. This is a magic weapon attack. On a critical hit against a target in an astral body (as with the [astral projection](compendium/spells/astral-projection.md) spell), Varakkta can cut the silvery cord that tethers the target to its material body, instead of dealing damage.
