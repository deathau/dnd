## Actions

***Multiattack.*** Belephaion makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 16 (2d10 + 5) piercing damage plus 5 (1d10) lightning damage.

***Claw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage.

***Lightning Breath (Recharge 5-6).*** Belephaion exhales lightning in a 60-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 55 (10d10) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

***Alter Shape.*** Belephaion magically transforms into an [eagle](compendium/bestiary/beast/eagle.md) or a [priest](compendium/bestiary/humanoid/priest.md), while retaining his alignment, damage immunities, hit points, Hit Dice, and Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores.
