## Actions

***Hoopak.*** *Melee  or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 40/160 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage, or 4 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage if Tem used the hoopak's sling to make a ranged attack.

***Taunt.*** Tem launches an infuriating barrage of insults at a creature she can see within 60 feet of her. If the target can hear Tem, it must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
