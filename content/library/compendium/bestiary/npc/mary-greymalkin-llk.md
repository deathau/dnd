***Fey Presence (Recharges after a Short Rest).*** Mary can cause each creature within 10 feet of her to succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or become [charmed](rules/conditions.md#Charmed) or [frightened](rules/conditions.md#Frightened) by her (Mary's choice) until the end of her next turn.

***Fey Ancestry.*** Mary has advantage on saving throws against being [charmed](rules/conditions.md#Charmed), and magic can't put her to sleep.
