***Spell Immunity.*** The snake horror is immune to three spells chosen by its creator. Typical immunities include [fireball](compendium/spells/fireball.md), [heat metal](compendium/spells/heat-metal.md), and [lightning bolt](compendium/spells/lightning-bolt.md).
