## Traits

***Fragile.*** If a critical hit is scored against the terracotta warrior, it shatters and is destroyed.

***Antimagic Susceptibility.*** The terracotta warrior is [incapacitated](rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated) while in the area of an [antimagic field](compendium/spells/antimagic-field.md). If targeted by [dispel magic](compendium/spells/dispel-magic.md), the terracotta warrior must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against the caster's spell save DC or fall [unconscious](rules/conditions.md#Unconscious) for 1 minute.

***False Appearance.*** While the terracotta warrior remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a terracotta statue.
