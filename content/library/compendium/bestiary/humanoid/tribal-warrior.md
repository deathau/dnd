# Tribal Warrior

_Source: Monster Manual p. 350, Princes of the Apocalypse, The Rise of Tiamat, Storm King's Thunder, Tales from the Yawning Portal, Tomb of Annihilation, Ghosts of Saltmarsh, Mythic Odysseys of Theros, Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden, The Tortle Package. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>_

Tribal warriors live beyond civilization, most often subsisting on fishing and hunting. Each tribe acts in accordance with the wishes of its chief, who is the greatest or oldest warrior of the tribe or a tribe member blessed by the gods.
