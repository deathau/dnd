## Traits

***Dragon Fanatic.*** The dragonclaw has advantage on saving throws against being [charmed](rules/conditions.md#Charmed) or [frightened](rules/conditions.md#Frightened). While the dragonclaw can see a dragon or higher-ranking Cult of the Dragon cultist friendly to it, the dragonclaw ignores the effects of being [charmed](rules/conditions.md#Charmed) or [frightened](rules/conditions.md#Frightened).

***Fanatic Advantage.*** Once per turn, if the dragonclaw makes a weapon attack with advantage on the attack roll and hits, it deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage.

***Pack Tactics.*** The dragonclaw has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the dragonclaw's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't [incapacitated](rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated).

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The dragonclaw attacks twice with its scimitar.

***Scimitar.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.
