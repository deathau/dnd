***Multiattack.*** The fey makes a number of attacks equal to half this spell's level (rounded down).

The fey can force one creature it can see within 10 feet of it to make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC. Unless the save succeeds, the target is [charmed](rules/conditions.md#Charmed) by you and the fey for 1 minute or until the target takes any damage.
