# Reef Shark

_Source: Monster Manual p. 336, Tomb of Annihilation, Ghosts of Saltmarsh, Critical Role: Call of the Netherdeep, Journeys through the Radiant Citadel, The Tortle Package. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span> and the Basic Rules (2014)_
