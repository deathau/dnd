_Source: Monster Manual p. 332, Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden, Critical Role: Call of the Netherdeep, Quests from the Infinite Staircase. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span> and the Basic Rules (2014)_
