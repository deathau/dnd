***Multiattack.*** The creature makes two attacks: one with its tentacles and one with its bite.

***Tentacles.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be [poisoned](rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) for 1 minute. Until this poison ends, the target is [paralyzed](rules/conditions.md#Paralyzed). The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the poison on itself on a success.

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) piercing damage.
