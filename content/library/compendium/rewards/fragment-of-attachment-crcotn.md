# Fragment of Attachment

_Fragment of Suffering_

## Benefit

You can't be [frightened](rules/conditions.md#Frightened) while within 10 feet of an ally. If you're already [frightened](rules/conditions.md#Frightened) and move within 10 feet of an ally, the [frightened](rules/conditions.md#Frightened) condition ends on you.

## Drawback

You have disadvantage on Wisdom and Charisma saving throws while you aren't within 10 feet of an ally.

_Source: Critical Role: Call of the Netherdeep p. 214_
