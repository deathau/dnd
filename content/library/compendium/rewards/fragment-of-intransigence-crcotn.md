# Fragment of Intransigence

_Fragment of Suffering_

## Benefit

Another creature can't force you to move anywhere you don't want to go.

## Drawback

You can't take the [Disengage](rules/actions.md#Disengage) or [Dodge](rules/actions.md#Dodge) action.

_Source: Critical Role: Call of the Netherdeep p. 214_
