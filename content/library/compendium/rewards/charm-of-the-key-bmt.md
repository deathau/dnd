# Charm of the Key

As a bonus action, you can touch a nonmagical melee weapon and imbue it with magic for 1 hour. For the duration, the weapon deals an extra `dice:1d8|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d8`) force damage on a hit and deals double damage to objects and structures. Once used three times, the charm vanishes from you.

_Source: The Book of Many Things p. 62_
