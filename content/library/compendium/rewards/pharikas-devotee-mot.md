# Pharika's Devotee

_Piety Trait_

_Piety 3+ Pharika trait_

As a devotee of Pharika, you have power over affliction. You can cast [ray of sickness](compendium/spells/ray-of-sickness.md) with this trait a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

_Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 70_
