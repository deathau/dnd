# Erebos's Votary

_Piety Trait_

_Piety 10+ Erebos trait_

You can cast [vampiric touch](compendium/spells/vampiric-touch.md) with this trait. Once you cast the spell in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

_Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 43_
