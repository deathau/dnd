# Dragonmark Appearance; Dragonmark Quirks

**Dragonmark Quirks**

`dice: [](dragonmark-appearance-dragonmark-quirks-erlw.md#^dragonmark-quirks)`

| dice: d8 | Quirk |
|----------|-------|
| 1 | Your dragonmark is unusually small or faint. |
| 2 | Your dragonmark is exceptionally large. |
