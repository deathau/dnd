The Book of Vengeful Knowledge - Sentient Book with a vengeful spirit within -
Grants the player one specific question answered about an enemy they face but the knowledge comes with a price: 1d4 roll
1- The book saps some of your health, 2 HP
2- The book becomes angry and that feeling seeps to you. You attack the closest being to you.
3- The book makes a play for your mind and forces you to move 30ft forward(or until you hit an object)
4- The book becomes vengeful and chooses your next attack target
