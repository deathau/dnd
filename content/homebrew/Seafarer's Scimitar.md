# Seafarer's Scimitar

_Magical melee weapon_

**Slot:** Off-Hand
**Attack:** +10 to hit
**Damage:** 1d6 + 3 slashing
**Property:** [[06-equipment#Finesse|Finesse]], [[06-equipment#Light|Light]], martial
**Weight:** 3 lbs.

You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon (already added above)

With a flick of the wrist and a few words, this blade can send a miniature tsunami towards an enemy once per day. Attack roll is the same as a normal scimitar, but damage is 2d6 to all creatures within a 15ft cone

Formerly owned by [[Captain Flint]]
